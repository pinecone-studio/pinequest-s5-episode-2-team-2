# References

Every medical or optical number in the app and the analysis tools comes from one of these
sources. Journal abstracts were checked on PubMed / Europe PMC.

0.40 mm ÷ 5.855 mm iris radius = 0.069 iris radii, the app's unit.

## Limits of these sources

- The Hirschberg ratios and the repeatability were measured with clinical cameras, not
  phones; the iris diameter was measured in people aged 10–80, not young children.
- The 8 prism dioptre target is for validating screening devices against a full eye exam; our
  thresholds stay `validated: false` until the app is checked against such exams.
